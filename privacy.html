Open POS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Open POS is a point-of-sale app that works entirely offline. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Who we are

Open POS is developed and published by Brand Builder. In this policy, "we" means the developer.

The short version

Open POS does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The app never sends your data anywhere. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud), if you have it turned on, may include app data.

The app's own code makes no network calls. From the next Android release, the Android build also does not have the INTERNET permission, so the operating system blocks it from connecting to any network. This operating-system block is Android-only: iOS has no equivalent setting.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

Products, sales, customers, expenses, settings and the expense-bill photos you add are kept on your device, in the app's private storage. The app never sends them to us or to anyone else.

You control this data:

  • Exports are files you choose to create and save. Where they go after that, for example a USB drive, your email or a cloud folder, is up to you.
  • Receipts. You can save or share a PDF receipt, share a receipt by SMS or email through your device's own apps, or copy it as text. When you share by SMS or email, your device's own Messages or Mail app, not Open POS, sends it.
  • Your phone's backup. If Google backup (Android) or iCloud or computer backup (iPhone and iPad) is turned on, your phone may include Open POS data in that backup. That is a setting of your phone, not of Open POS.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device. If your phone's own backup is on, that backup may restore the data when you reinstall the app.

Permissions on Android

The table below lists the permissions in the next Android release.

Next Android release
PermissionWhy it is used
CameraTo photograph an expense bill, or choose an existing photo from your library, only when you tap to do so. Photos are kept on your device.
Bluetooth (scan/connect)To find and connect to Bluetooth receipt printers. In this release, Bluetooth scanning on Android 12 and newer is declared as not used for location.
Location (Android 11 and older only)Android 11 and older require this permission before an app can search for Bluetooth devices. Open POS uses it only to find receipt printers. It never reads or stores your location, and with no internet permission in this release, it could not send it anywhere. On Android 12 and newer, this permission is not requested.
Storage (Android 12 and older only)To save and open CSV and PDF files you choose.
USBDeclared by the receipt-printer library the app uses. Open POS does not currently print over USB.

Android store versions from May 2026 differ from this table. They also declare notification, alarm, wake-lock, run-at-startup, vibration and nearby Wi-Fi devices (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES) permissions, left over from features the app does not use. Their Bluetooth permissions are declared differently, and they have no location permission. The app never sends notifications, and its own code makes no network calls. These leftover permissions are removed in the next release. The camera is used only for expense bill photos.

Permissions on iPhone and iPad

On iOS, Open POS asks for access to the camera and the photo library (to add an expense-bill photo, only when you tap to do so) and to Bluetooth (to find and connect to Bluetooth receipt printers).

The May 2026 iOS build also declares local-network access and Bonjour printer services, which the next iOS build removes.

iOS has no internet permission that an app can leave out, so the operating-system network block described above applies to Android only, from the next Android release. On iOS, the protection is that the app's own code makes no network calls, and the source code is public for anyone to check.

Children

Open POS is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.
