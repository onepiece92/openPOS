Square and Open POS, side by side

Square is a POS with its own card processing and card readers. You sign up for a Square account. It also offers online store tools. For many shops that is exactly right.

Open POS is a different kind of tool. There is no account and no server at all. Sales are stored on the device, and the app never sends them anywhere. The whole app is free and open source under the MIT license. It does not process cards.

Which one fits your shop

Open POS fits if

you run one counter or one stall, mostly take cash or already have a card reader, and want no account, no subscription and no ads. It works the same with or without a signal.

Square fits if

you want built-in card processing or an online store. Open POS does not do those things.

Switching over

Install Open POS, pick your country and currency, and import your products from a CSV file. You may need to match the column names to the Open POS import format first.

What you get in Open POS

A product grid with categories and favorites, cash sales with change, whole-order discounts, held tickets, tables, customers, expenses, and reports for today, this week or this month. Tax can be on or off per product. PDF receipts can be saved or shared, or copied as text. Data can be exported to CSV. Bluetooth receipt printing is coming.

Limits to know first

One device is one register. There are no staff logins, and devices do not sync with each other. Card sales are recorded, not processed.

Privacy you can check

The app's own code makes no network calls: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no cloud services. The source code is public on GitHub. From the next release onwards, the Android build also has no internet permission, so the operating system itself blocks any connection. How to check it.

Questions about switching

Is Open POS affiliated with Square?

No. Square is a trademark of Block, Inc. Open POS is an independent, open-source app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Square or Block, Inc. We name Square here only to compare the two.

Can Open POS take card payments?

No. Open POS does not process cards. You can record card sales taken on your own card reader, but the reader needs its own connection and its own account with its processor. If built-in card processing is what you need, Square, which has its own card processing, may be a better fit.

Do I need an account to use Open POS?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Install the app and start selling.

Does Open POS work with no internet?

Yes. Everything in the app works with no signal, every time: the product grid, cash sales with change, held tickets, tables, customers, expenses and reports.

Can several phones share the same sales?

No. Each phone or tablet is its own register, with its own data, and devices do not sync. There are also no staff logins. If you need several devices in sync, the same team makes RebuzzPOS, a separate online POS; you never need it to use Open POS.

Can I move my products over from Square?

Open POS imports products from a CSV file. There is no direct Square importer, so you may need to export your items from Square and rename the columns in a spreadsheet to match the Open POS import format.

Is Open POS on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It is on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
