Why a point of sale without internet?

Most "free" POS apps are a front door to a cloud account. Open POS is a cash register app that lives entirely on your phone.

Sell when the signal drops

A basement shop, a farmers market, a food truck parked out of range, a power outage that takes the router down. The register keeps ringing up cash sales, because the app never needed a connection. A card reader needs its own.

No subscription, no monthly bill

No subscription, no per-device fee, no trial that runs out. There is no account to sign up for, and none to lose access to. Why it costs nothing.

Your numbers stay yours

Sales, customers and expenses are kept on your device, and the app never sends them anywhere. We never see them.

Privacy you can check, not just trust

Anyone can write "we respect your privacy". Open POS is a private POS app with no tracking, built so you can look for yourself.

Today

The app makes no network calls

The app's own code makes no network calls. It never included analytics or cloud services, and it has no ads and no crash reporting. The full source code is public on GitHub, so you or a friend who codes can read it for yourself.

The app never sends your data anywhere. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud), if you have it turned on, may include app data.

Next release

Android itself blocks the network

From the next release onwards, the Android build has no internet permission. Without it, the operating system does not let the app open any connection, even by mistake. You can verify it on the release file with one command.

from the next Android release: check the APK yourself
$ aapt dump permissions \
    open-pos.apk | grep INTERNET
# no output = no internet permission
✓ nothing to send it with

Applies to release APKs from the next release onwards. Read the full privacy policy.

Questions shop owners ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Open POS itself has no paid tier, no trial, no ads and no in-app purchases. Open POS is open source under the MIT license, and it will stay free.

How do you make money?

Not from Open POS. The same team makes RebuzzPOS, a separate online POS with 40+ additional features for businesses that need sync across devices.

The Support screen in Open POS links to it. Open POS exchanges no data with it, and you never need it to use Open POS.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, always. There is no login and no server, so a dropped connection, airplane mode or a market with no signal never stops the app. Card sales are different: your card reader needs its own connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Install the app, pick your country and currency, add your products and you are ready. No email, no phone number, no password.

What if my phone breaks or gets lost?

The app never sends your data anywhere, so there is no Open POS cloud copy. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud), if you have it turned on, may include app data, but keep your own copy too. Export your products and records to CSV files and save them somewhere safe: a USB drive, your own email or a cloud folder you choose. On a new phone, install Open POS and import the CSV files.

A one-file backup and restore is coming in an update.

Which printers does it work with?

Save or share a PDF receipt; you can print the PDF from another app. You can also copy the receipt as text. Direct printing to Bluetooth receipt printers is coming soon.

Does it take card payments?

No. Open POS does not process or take card payments. Take the payment on your own card terminal, then record the sale in Open POS as paid by card.

Does it work for a restaurant with tables?

Yes. Table management lets you open an order for each table, hold it while guests eat, and close it when they pay.

Can several phones share the same sales?

No. Each phone or tablet is its own register, with its own data, and there are no staff logins. That is what keeps everything offline. If you need several devices in sync, RebuzzPOS is built for that.

Is there a cash register app with no subscription?

Yes, this one. Open POS has no subscription, no trial and no in-app purchases, on Android and on iPhone and iPad. See what is included for free.

Is Open POS open source?

Yes. The full source code is public on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can read it, check it and build it.

Does it work for a food truck or farmers market?

Yes. The app itself never needs a signal. It gives change on cash sales and lets you record card sales you took on your own reader (the reader needs its own connection). See how it works at the market.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Open POS is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Will I lose sales if I have no signal?

Open POS never needs a connection, so the register, cash sales and your sales record keep working at a market, a festival or a food truck with no bars. Nothing waits to upload. If you take cards, your card reader needs its own connection, and Open POS cannot change that.

Can the price go up later?

Open POS is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no subscription, no per-location fee and no paid plan to be moved onto, so there is no monthly bill that can be raised. You can read the source code on GitHub.

Can my account be locked or frozen?

Open POS has no account to lock. There is no sign-up, login or password, so nobody can lock you out of your register, your products or your sales history. Open POS does not handle payouts of any kind.

Are there paid add-ons or features locked behind a higher plan?

No. Everything in Open POS is included for free: discounts, held tickets, tables, customers, expenses, reports for today, this week or this month, and CSV export. There are no tiers and nothing to unlock. Open POS does not do everything, though. For example, it has no staff logins. If you outgrow it, the same team makes RebuzzPOS, a separate online POS with 40+ additional features; you never need it to use Open POS.

How long does setup take?

Install the app, pick your country and currency, and add your products and categories. There is no sign-up, no email and no verification step to wait for, so you can ring up a first sale in minutes. A card reader, if you use one, has its own setup.

Am I tied to one card reader company?

No. Open POS does not take card payments, so it is not tied to any reader. You take the payment on your own reader, then record the sale in Open POS as paid by card. The reader needs its own connection, and its company sets its own terms.
