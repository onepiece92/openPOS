Loyverse and Open POS, side by side

Loyverse POS is a popular point of sale with a free core app. It is a cloud system: you sign up for an account, and your sales go to an online Back Office that you open in a web browser. In Loyverse's own words, you can "keep recording sales even when offline", and "offline sales sync to the Back Office when you're back online". It supports more than one store and a customer rewards program, and "optional paid add-ons are available". Employee management is one of them: Loyverse's pricing page lists it at $5 per employee per month.

Open POS takes a different approach. There is no account and no server at all. Sales are stored on the device, and the app never sends them anywhere. The whole app is free and open source under the MIT license.

Which one fits your shop

Open POS fits if

you run one counter or one stall, you don't want an account or a cloud service, and you want an app that never sends your sales anywhere. It works the same with or without a signal.

Loyverse fits if

you need several stores or devices in sync, staff logins, a rewards program or a web back office you can open from anywhere. Open POS does not do those things.

Switching over

Install Open POS, pick your country and currency, and import your products from a CSV file. You may need to match the column names to the Open POS import format first.

Privacy you can check

The app's own code makes no network calls: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no cloud services. The source code is public on GitHub. From the next release onwards, the Android build also has no internet permission, so the operating system itself blocks any connection. How to check it.

Questions about switching

Is Open POS affiliated with Loyverse?

No. Loyverse is a trademark of its owner. Open POS is an independent, open-source app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Loyverse. We name it here only to compare the two.

Do I need an account to use Open POS?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Install the app and start selling.

Can I move my products over from Loyverse?

Open POS imports products from a CSV file. There is no direct Loyverse importer, so you may need to rename columns in a spreadsheet to match the Open POS import format.

Can several phones share the same sales, like a cloud POS?

No. Each phone or tablet is its own register, with its own data. That is what keeps everything offline. If you need several devices in sync, a cloud POS such as Loyverse is a better fit, as is the same team's separate online POS, RebuzzPOS; you never need it to use Open POS.

Is Open POS on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It is on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
